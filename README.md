## 🚀 How to Open the 3D Viewer

You have two instant options to open and experience the 3D model:

### Option 1: Direct in Your Browser (No Server Needed)
Simply open the file in your preferred web browser (Google Chrome, Safari, or Microsoft Edge):
- **Finder:** Navigate to `/Users/sanjay/house-3d-viewer` and double-click `index.html`.
- **Or via Terminal:**
  ```bash
  open /Users/sanjay/house-3d-viewer/index.html
  ```

### Option 2: Local Web Server (Recommended for Best Performance)
In your terminal, run:
```bash
cd /Users/sanjay/house-3d-viewer
python3 -m http.server 8080
```
Then open `http://localhost:8080` in your web browser.

## 🎮 Interactive Controls & Features

### 1. View & Floor Cutaway Modes
- **🏛️ Full Villa:** Complete exterior elevation with weather-proof off-white paint, charcoal grey accents, teak wood-plastic composite (WPC) louvers, balcony box frames, and terrace parapet.
- **🛋️ Ground Floor:** Isolates the ground floor to inspect room arrangements (Commercial Shutter, Car Portico, Living Room, Puja Room, Dining, Kitchen, Rear Utility, Master Bedroom & Attached Toilet).
- **🛏️ First Floor (Duplex):** Isolates the upper floor to showcase the **Double-Height Cutout Void** overlooking the ground floor living room, family lounge, study/office, bedroom suites, and front balcony.
- **🌅 Terrace Floor:** Roof terrace layout with staircase mumty (headroom tower), Sintex water tank, modern pergola over the void, and parapet walls.
- **✨ Duplex Void View:** Teleports the camera to the first-floor glass railing, looking down into the two-story living room chandelier below.

### 2. Camera Presets
- **Front Elevation:** True South-facing street elevation view.
- **3D Isometric:** High-angle perspective showing facade and depth.
- **Top-Down Plan:** 2D architectural blueprint layout view.
- **Car Portico & Lobby:** Ground-level entry perspective.
- **Duplex High Void:** Perspective from the upper corridor looking down into the living hall.
- **North Rear:** Backyard service area and rear balcony.

### 3. Lighting & Ambience Modes
- **☀️ Tropical Day:** Crisp daytime sunlight matching Telangana's natural latitude.
- **🌇 Golden Hour:** Warm evening sunset glow.
- **🌙 Architectural Night:** Warm 3000K up-and-down spotlights on feature pillars, under-slab LED strip lighting, and glowing interior windows.

### 4. Interactive Room Inspector
- **Hover or Click on any room floor** to highlight it in gold and view its exact dimensions, estimated area, Vastu orientation, and architect notes in the floating details card.
